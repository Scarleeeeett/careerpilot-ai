# Evidence rules

## Source hierarchy

Use the strongest available source:

1. Laws, regulators, standards bodies, government notices
2. Company filings, product pages, price lists, technical documents
3. Customer or partner announcements and government cases
4. Research reports with a disclosed method
5. Reputable media
6. Aggregators, reposts, social posts, search snippets

Use lower-tier sources only when stronger sources do not disclose the fact. Say so.

## Claim ledger

Record volatile or critical claims with:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Claim | Exact statement being supported |
| Value and unit | Preserve original unit |
| Period | Point-in-time, month, quarter, or year |
| Geography | China, global, city, project, etc. |
| Scope | Company, model, order, shipment, fleet, route, case |
| Source | Direct URL and title |
| Source type | Official, government, research, media |
| Confidence | High, medium, or low |
| Limitation | Missing definition or non-comparable element |

## Required distinctions

- Order is not shipment.
- Shipment is not production capacity or active fleet.
- Cumulative deployment is not annual share.
- List price is not transaction price, subscription cost, or TCO.
- Pilot, road test, commercial demonstration, and commercial operation are different stages.
- A claimed cost reduction is a case claim unless independently verified with a disclosed baseline.
- A specification is nominal unless operating conditions are stated.

## Freshness and conflict

Browse for prices, executives, financing, regulation, standards, specifications, market share, shipments, coverage, cases, and current strategy. Put `as of YYYY-MM-DD` in the report. If sources conflict, prefer the more authoritative and newer source, show both definitions, or mark the fact unresolved.

## Confidence

- **High**: regulator, filing, standard, or directly verifiable official specification.
- **Medium**: official company claim, partner case, government case quoting a company, or reputable research with incomplete method.
- **Low**: media estimate, indirect quotation, unclear period, or conflicting scope.

Always explain the limitation; confidence alone is insufficient.
