# Output template

## Quick answer

```markdown
# 直接结论
一句话回答用户问题。

## 分类或定义
给出口径与必要解释。

## 关键链路
产业链、流程、公式或比较表。

## 对岗位或面试的意义
3 至 5 个可执行判断。

## 来源与限制
在对应事实附近引用来源，并说明未披露或不可比内容。
```

## Standard report

```markdown
# [Company] [Role] industry brief

Data updated: YYYY-MM-DD
Scope and assumptions: ...

## 岗位与业务
### 核心结论
### 输入与研究口径
### 业务归属判断（仅在未确认时）
### JD requirement map
### Role mapping

## 行业与产品
### Industry classification and boundaries
### Demand, users, workflow, and value chain
### Products and technology stack
### Company position and business model
### Competitors and representative products
### Economics and key metrics
### Policy, safety, and risks

## 核心能力
### Role-critical evaluation or operating framework
### Key terminology

## 面试使用
### Interview questions and answer frameworks
### Questions for the interviewer
### Sources, claim limitations, and open questions
```

Do not render all subsections as equal-weight navigation items. The four chapter headings are the first level; their subsections are the second level. Omit irrelevant subsections rather than leaving empty shells.

The input/scope subsection records company, role, geography, research date, output format source, business-line status, completed clarification, user hypotheses, and unresolved facts. When the business line is unknown, the business-attribution subsection uses:

```text
Candidate scope | Fit | Supporting clues | Missing/contradictory evidence | Preparation implication
```

## Deep additions

Add only when useful:

- Customer workflow diagrams
- Case cards with old/new process and limitations
- Product comparison matrix
- TCO or unit economics worksheet
- Historical timeline
- Claim ledger
- Policy-stage table
- Questions requiring recruiter confirmation

## HTML structure

Use the grouped chapter order above. Make terminology searchable when there are more than 15 terms. Put volatile facts in a structured data file when ongoing updates are expected.
