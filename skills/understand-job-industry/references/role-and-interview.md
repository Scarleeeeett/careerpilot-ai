# Role and interview mapping

## Role mapping

Map the role through six questions:

1. Which user and workflow does it serve?
2. Which system or product module does it own?
3. Which decisions recur daily?
4. Which teams, suppliers, and customers are interfaces?
5. Which operational and business metrics define success?
6. Which failures have the highest cost?

For product roles, cover inputs, outputs, capability boundaries, exceptions, permissions, logs, rollout, and feedback. For strategy or operations, cover supply, demand, utilization, delivery, contracts, and economics. For data or algorithm roles, cover data generation, labels, offline evaluation, online monitoring, and failure attribution.

## Interview package

Produce:

- 30-second company and industry answer
- 60- to 90-second industry answer
- 5 to 10 likely questions
- Framework, evidence, limitation, and likely follow-up for each
- 3 interviewer questions revealing business stage and role scope

## Question families

- Why this industry and why now?
- Where is the company in the value chain?
- Who pays, uses, and benefits?
- How does the company make money?
- Which competitor is most comparable?
- Which product or scenario is strongest?
- What determines unit economics?
- What prevents adoption?
- Which policy or safety issue constrains growth?
- What would this role improve first?

## Answer standard

```text
Conclusion → framework → 2 or 3 facts → limitation → role implication
```

Avoid market-size recitals without workflow, scope, commercial constraints, and role decisions.
